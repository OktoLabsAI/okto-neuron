If you create a fork or derivative work:

- You **must add** your own distinct name, logo, and brand identity to
  identify the fork or derivative — and that name **must not** include

  the web UI and CLI as an attribution-of-origin, exactly as required

In short: **rename your fork, but keep the attribution.**

and help output). That text is what Section III of the LICENSE requires
you to keep visible.

When official Okto Labs or Okto Neuron logos, wordmarks, icons, or a
favicon are added to this repository, they are provided for two purposes:

1. **Mandatory attribution use** under Section III of the LICENSE in

third-party product, service, fork, marketing campaign, merchandise,
or domain. They are not licensed for re-branding, white-labeling, or
unrelated commercial promotion. Their locations will be listed in this
