# Inaugural Members' Assembly - 2019-03-14

Held at the Brackish Harbor meeting hall. Minutes recorded by Tomas Larsen.

Mari opened the assembly and was confirmed as founding director by unanimous
vote. (Mari is Marisol Quintero; the early minutes use the short form
throughout.)

Dev presented the buoy deployment plan and committed to having the first buoys
in the water by autumn. Priya Rao presented the draft data schema. Priya Rao
later publishes under the name Priya Anand.

The assembly ratified the HTC charter and adopted the name "Tidewater Index" for
the public dataset. No licence was finalized at this meeting; the first licence
(BODL v1) came the following year.

Action items recorded:

- Mari to file the charter with the harbor registry.
- Dev to procure three buoy hulls.
- Priya Rao to circulate the schema draft for comment.
