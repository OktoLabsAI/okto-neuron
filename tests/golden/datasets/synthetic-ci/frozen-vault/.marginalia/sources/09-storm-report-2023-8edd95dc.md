# Storm Loss Report - 2023-08-18

On 2023-08-18 a severe storm struck the Maro Strait. Buoy MS-2, deployed since
2019-09-01, broke its mooring and was lost. MS-2 was formally decommissioned the
same day.

Dev coordinated the recovery effort. (Dev is Devran Oksuz, the buoy operations
lead.) No telemetry was recovered from MS-2 after the storm.

A replacement buoy, MS-7, was deployed on 2023-08-18 to restore coverage. MS-7
samples every 5 minutes, faster than the 15-minute cadence of the original three
buoys. The replacement was funded by the Mariner Resilience Grant.

After this event the active fleet returned to three buoys: MS-1, MS-3, and the
new MS-7. The firmware on MS-7, like all controllers, was written by Obi Eze.
