# Director Handover Note - 2023-01-09

This note records the transfer of the HTC directorship.

Marisol Quintero (founding director since 2019) stepped down effective
2023-01-09. Greta Sandoval was appointed as the second director of the Halverson
Tidewater Collective on the same day.

Greta Sandoval inherits oversight of all three programs: Buoy Operations (led by
Devran Oksuz), Data Platform (led by Priya Anand), and Community Trust
(coordinated by Tomas Larsen).

Marisol's parting priority, recorded here for the new director, was to secure
replacement funding for the aging buoy fleet. That priority was met later in
2023 through the Mariner Resilience Grant.

Greta Sandoval is the current director as of this record. There is no further
directorship change after 2023.
