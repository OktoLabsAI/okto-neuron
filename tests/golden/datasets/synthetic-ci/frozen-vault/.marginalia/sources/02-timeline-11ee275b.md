# HTC Timeline

A chronological record of governance and program milestones.

- **2019-03-14** - HTC charter ratified; Marisol Quintero becomes founding director.
- **2019-09-01** - First three buoys deployed in the Maro Strait (buoys MS-1, MS-2, MS-3).
- **2020-06-15** - Tidewater Index v1 published under the Brackish Open Data Licence v1.
- **2021-02-10** - Priya Anand promoted from data platform engineer to data platform lead.
- **2021-11-30** - Cape Verriden field office opened; Rashid Farouk appointed vessel liaison.
- **2022-04-22** - Brackish Open Data Licence upgraded from v1 to v2.
- **2023-01-09** - Marisol Quintero steps down as director; Greta Sandoval becomes the second director.
- **2023-08-18** - Buoy MS-2 lost in a storm and decommissioned; replacement buoy MS-7 deployed.
- **2024-03-03** - Yusuf Karim joins as junior data analyst.
- **2024-10-12** - Tidewater Index v2 published, switching the salinity unit to practical salinity units (PSU).

Devran Oksuz has been buoy operations lead continuously since the 2019 founding.
