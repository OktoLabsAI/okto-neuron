# Membership Rules

Membership in the Halverson Tidewater Collective is open to any vessel owner who
contributes data.

- A member must log **at least one tide reading per quarter** to stay active.
- Membership grants voting rights at the annual members' assembly.
- Members may use the Tidewater Index under the Brackish Open Data Licence v2.

As of the 2024 roster, HTC has **142 active member vessels**. Membership is
coordinated by the Community Trust program under Tomas Larsen.

New vessels in the Cape Verriden region register through Rashid Farouk, the
vessel liaison for that field office.

Membership does not require a fee. The only obligation is the quarterly tide
reading.
