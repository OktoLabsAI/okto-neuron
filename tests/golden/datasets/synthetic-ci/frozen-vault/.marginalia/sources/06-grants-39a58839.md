# Grants Ledger

The Community Trust program funds HTC operations through external grants. All
grants are administered by Hana Vesely.

| Grant | Year | Amount | Purpose |
|-------|------|--------|---------|
| Saltmarsh Fund | 2020 | 48,000 credits | Initial buoy deployment |
| Tidewater Open Data Grant | 2021 | 65,000 credits | Data platform build-out |
| Verriden Expansion Grant | 2021 | 30,000 credits | Cape Verriden field office |
| Mariner Resilience Grant | 2023 | 90,000 credits | Storm-loss buoy replacement |

The single largest grant HTC has received is the Mariner Resilience Grant at
90,000 credits, awarded in 2023 after buoy MS-2 was lost.

Total grant funding across all four grants is 233,000 credits.

The Mariner Resilience Grant directly funded the deployment of replacement buoy
MS-7. The Verriden Expansion Grant paid for the field office where Rashid Farouk
serves as vessel liaison.
