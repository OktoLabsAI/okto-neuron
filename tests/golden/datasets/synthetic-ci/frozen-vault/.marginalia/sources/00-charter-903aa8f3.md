# Halverson Tidewater Collective - Charter

The Halverson Tidewater Collective (HTC) is a coastal-sensor data cooperative
founded in 2019. HTC operates a network of tide and salinity buoys along the
Maro Strait and publishes an open dataset called the **Tidewater Index**.

HTC is headquartered in the town of Brackish Harbor. A second field office
opened later in Cape Verriden.

The Collective is organized into three programs:

- **Buoy Operations** - deploys and maintains the physical sensor buoys.
- **Data Platform** - runs the ingest pipeline and publishes the Tidewater Index.
- **Community Trust** - manages membership, grants, and the public data licence.

The Tidewater Index is released under the Brackish Open Data Licence (BODL),
version 2. Membership in the Collective is open to any vessel owner who logs at
least one tide reading per quarter.

The founding director of HTC was Marisol Quintero. The charter was ratified on
2019-03-14 at the inaugural members' assembly in Brackish Harbor.
