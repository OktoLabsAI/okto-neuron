# HTC People Directory

This directory lists active members and staff of the Halverson Tidewater
Collective. Names sometimes appear in shortened or married forms elsewhere in
the records; the canonical full name is given first.

- **Marisol Quintero** - founding director (2019-2023). Often referred to simply
  as "Mari" in early meeting notes.
- **Devran Oksuz** - buoy operations lead. Goes by "Dev" in field logs.
- **Priya Anand** - data platform engineer; later platform lead. Maiden name
  Priya Rao appears in pre-2021 records.
- **Tomas Larsen** - community trust coordinator.
- **Nadia Belmonte** - salinity calibration specialist.
- **Obi Eze** - embedded-firmware contractor for the buoy controllers.
- **Hana Vesely** - grants officer.
- **Rashid Farouk** - vessel liaison for the Cape Verriden office.
- **Greta Sandoval** - second director of HTC, from 2023.
- **Yusuf Karim** - junior data analyst, joined 2024.

Devran Oksuz and Priya Anand co-authored the original Tidewater Index schema.
Nadia Belmonte reports to the buoy operations lead.
