# Cape Verriden Field Office

The Cape Verriden field office is the Halverson Tidewater Collective's second
location. It opened on 2021-11-30, funded by the Verriden Expansion Grant.

Cape Verriden is a satellite office, not the headquarters. The HTC headquarters
remains in Brackish Harbor, where the Collective was founded in 2019. Cape
Verriden handles vessel registration for the eastern strait only.

Rashid Farouk is the vessel liaison based at Cape Verriden. He is the first point
of contact for vessel owners registering in the eastern strait.

The office has no buoy-operations staff of its own; all buoys are managed from
Brackish Harbor by Devran Oksuz's team. Cape Verriden's role is community and
membership outreach.
