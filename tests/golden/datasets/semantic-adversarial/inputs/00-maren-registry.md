# Rivermark registry

Dr. Maren Vale is a harbor cartographer. Older harbor logs use the short name M. Vale for the same
person; M. Vale and Dr. Maren Vale are one person, not two people.

Rivermark is the birthplace of Dr. Maren Vale. Maren now lives in Rivermark.

The phrase "harbor cartographer" is Maren's role. It is a literal role description, not a second
person, organization, place, or graph endpoint.
